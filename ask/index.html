Foundation

The platform

Optym's first and final mile optimization and execution platform handles pickup and delivery for LTL carriers: a React and TypeScript planner and dispatcher web app, a React Native driver app, Node.js and .NET backends, Azure SQL, and Kubernetes.

I developed its 2017 MVP and led the 2018 rebuild into a cloud native, multi tenant platform. It is in production at 11 LTL carriers in the US and Canada, including Southeastern Freight Lines, Saia, and Southwestern Motor Transport, supporting about 20,000 drivers and about 1,000 planners and dispatchers at roughly a million transactions per hour and 99.7% uptime. The platform won an SDCE Top Supply Chain Project award for the deployment at Saia.

Releases are monthly.

About

How I work

VP of Product Development and Engineering in Dallas. I lead 30 people across Dallas and Bengaluru through 4 engineering managers, and I own engineering, the product roadmap, and the business unit P&L. I have hired more than 20 people across MAQ Software and Optym, most of whom were later promoted, and mentored about 20.

The product work starts with customers. I spend about 30% of my time with users at carrier terminals and with drivers. Before building the driver app I spent 3 weeks on the road with drivers. I build working prototypes with customers during discovery and take them through design, build, and production. I stay in the architecture and the code.

I support sales engineering and RFP responses, then lead implementation and ROI realization. I have led every carrier deployment since 2018. Each runs 1 to 6 months, with integrations into legacy AS400 and custom TMS systems designed so carriers do as little work as possible. Carriers got fewer routes, fewer miles, nearly zero missed appointments, fewer returns, and a 75% cut in planning time.

Earlier at MAQ Software I built applications for Microsoft, Starbucks, and Amazon, and led a team of 10 from 2015.
